About Frozen Trendy, comfortable, and durable clothing made for the way kids actually play.

Frozen started in 2019 with a simple idea: kids' clothing should keep up with kids, not the other way around. We were tired of outfits that faded after two washes or fell apart at the playground, so we set out to build a brand that pairs playful design with genuinely durable materials.

Today we design and ship clothing for babies, girls, and boys — from everyday basics to special-occasion pieces — sourced from ethically certified factories and tested by the toughest critics we know: actual kids.

Quality First Every fabric is tested for durability, breathability, and softness before it reaches our shelves.
Ethically Made We partner exclusively with OEKO-TEX certified factories that put fair labor practices first.
Built to Play Reinforced seams and pre-shrunk fits mean our clothes survive the playground, not just the closet.
