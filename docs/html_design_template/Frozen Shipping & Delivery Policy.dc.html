Shipping & Delivery Policy Last updated: September 12, 2026
Shipping Options

We offer Standard Delivery (5-7 business days), Express Delivery (2-3 business days), and Overnight Delivery (next business day). Delivery times are estimates and may vary by destination.

Shipping Costs

Orders over $99 qualify for free standard shipping. Orders under $99 incur a flat $6.99 standard shipping fee. Express and overnight rates are calculated at checkout.

Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days of being placed. You will receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information once your order ships.

International Shipping

We currently ship to over 30 countries. International orders may be subject to customs fees and import duties, which are the responsibility of the recipient.

Delivery Issues

If your package is lost, damaged, or delayed, please contact our support team at support@frozen-kids.com and we'll work with you to resolve the issue promptly.
